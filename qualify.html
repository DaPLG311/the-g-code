What Qualifies

Three things get an idea built. Titles aren't one of them.

01

A real problem

Something a specific person actually struggles with — a job that takes too long, money left on the table, a process held together with tape. If the problem is real, the build has a spine.

02

A person behind it

An owner, not a bystander. You don't need to be technical — you need to care enough to answer questions, make decisions, and show up for the work sessions that shape it.

03

A first version worth building

Not the ten-year vision — the Day One version. Something real we can put in front of users, customers, or backers fast, and grow from there.

The Checklist

Run yourself through it. Honestly.

  • I can describe the problem in one or two sentences.
  • I know who feels this problem — even if it's just me and people like me.
  • I'm ready to talk to the person who would build it, not a sales rep.
  • I can make decisions about my own idea when I'm asked to.
  • I want a real first version — not a deck about one.

Checked most of them? You're closer than most people who call themselves founders.
