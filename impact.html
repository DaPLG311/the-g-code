Small-Business Access

Independent companies deserve to compete.

Day One MVP™ helps independent companies compete with larger teams, larger budgets, and better technology — by giving them access to the same quality of strategy, systems, and tools. These are the kinds of work we help with.

  • Better websites
  • Customer systems
  • Automation
  • Sales processes
  • Marketing
  • Media
  • Training
  • AI tools
  • Internal operations
Nonprofit Technology

Mission-driven work deserves better systems.

For mission-driven organizations doing meaningful work without the systems, funding, or support they deserve, Day One MVP™ can help build the infrastructure that lets them serve more people. These are the kinds of work we help with.

Youth In Technology

Young people belong in technology.

We want to help young people with talent, imagination, and ambition explore technology — and see that it isn't something happening somewhere else, controlled by people they'll never meet. These are the areas they can explore.

  • AI
  • Product development
  • Coding
  • Website building
  • Application design
  • Sales
  • Marketing
  • Audio
  • Video
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Creative technology
  • Responsible technology use
Young people should not only consume the future. They should have the opportunity to build it.
