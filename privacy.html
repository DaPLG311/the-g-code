Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Day One MVP™ ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit dayonemvp.com or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you email or call us about a project, you share details like your name, email address, phone number, and what you tell us about your idea.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs standard request data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and reliability.
  • Fonts. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of serving those files.

This site does not use advertising cookies or sell your data. We do not run on-site forms that store your information in a database — inquiries reach us directly by email or phone.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and scope, quote, or deliver a project.
  • To communicate with you about work you've asked us to do.
  • To keep the site secure and working properly.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only with service providers that help us operate (for example, our hosting provider), or when required by law.

Data retention

We keep inquiry communications only as long as needed to do the work and maintain normal business records, then delete them on request.

Your choices & rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you've shared with us. To do so, contact us using the details below. You can also stop sharing information at any time by not contacting us.

Children

This site is intended for adults and business owners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
