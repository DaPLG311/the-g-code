What Happens Next

Four moves from message to work in motion.

  1. You send one message. An email to Pepper or Wiz. No form maze, no discovery-call funnel, no "let's find time to find time."
  2. They find the fit. A few questions to understand the problem, who it's for, and what a real first version looks like.
  3. You get the Operated Call™. A working session with the operator shaping the build — and when the work calls for it, you watch it happen live.
  4. The build starts. You leave the call with the project moving, not with a proposal to think about.
