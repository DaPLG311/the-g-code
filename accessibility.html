Accessibility

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Day One MVP™ wants dayonemvp.com to be usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technology. Accessibility is part of how we build — for this site and for the sites we ship for clients.

What we do

  • Readable type. Body text is set at comfortable sizes with generous line height, and headings scale down cleanly on small screens.
  • Contrast. Text and interactive elements are designed to stand out clearly against the dark background.
  • Reduced motion. Animations and scroll effects respect your system's reduced-motion preference — turn it on and the site calms down.
  • Keyboard navigation. Navigation, links, and buttons are reachable and operable by keyboard.
  • Semantic structure. Pages use meaningful headings, landmarks, and alt text on images so screen readers can make sense of them.

Work in progress

Accessibility is never finished. We review pages as we build and improve them as we learn — and we would rather hear about a problem than have you struggle with it silently.

Found an issue?

If any part of this site is hard to use with your setup — screen reader, keyboard, magnification, anything — tell us. Email jack@dayonemvp.com with the page and what happened, and we'll work on a fix.
